# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Electronic music fans discovering ROY KNOX for the first time, existing listeners checking tour dates and merch, and industry contacts (booking, demos). They arrive from social, festival lineups, or label pages — usually on mobile, often at night, in a high-energy listening context.

## Product Purpose

A single-scroll artist landing page that channels ROY KNOX's blue-flame identity: emotional melodub bass music with metal-adjacent visual aggression. The page must feel like a live show build-up — not a generic EDM template — while surfacing music, live dates, merch, and label credibility.

## Brand Personality

**Volatile. Melodic. Ritual.**

Emotional catharsis through bass — the contrast between tender melody and explosive drops. Visual language borrows from Sleep Token's cinematic restraint and BMTH's typographic aggression, filtered through Roy's signature blue flame and burning piano motif.

## Anti-references

- Generic purple-gradient EDM producer sites
- Cream/sand SaaS landing pages
- Identical icon-card feature grids
- Wix-style artist templates (current royknoxmusic.com)
- Over-rounded "friendly startup" UI
- Neon cyberpunk cliché without the flame/piano identity

## Design Principles

1. **Flame before filler** — every section earns its place in the narrative arc (arrive → feel → listen → see live → wear it).
2. **Contrast is the instrument** — deep void backgrounds against electric blue flame; silence before the drop.
3. **Typography as performance** — headlines hit like stage signage; body stays legible under strobe.
4. **Motion with purpose** — choreographed like a build-up, not decoration on scroll.
5. **Credibility through labels** — the marquee of imprints is proof, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (AAA for body text where feasible). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks. Keyboard-navigable shop/show CTAs. Sufficient contrast on all flame-tinted surfaces.
